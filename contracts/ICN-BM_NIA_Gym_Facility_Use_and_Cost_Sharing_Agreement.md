## 1. Parties & Recitals

This Gymnasium Facility Use & Cost-Sharing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the Islamic Center of Nashville at Bellevue Mosque, a Tennessee nonprofit corporation ("ICN Bellevue" or "Licensor"), and Nashville International Academy ("NIA" or "Licensee"), collectively the "Parties."

WHEREAS, ICN Bellevue owns and operates a multi-purpose gymnasium facility of approximately 10,000 square feet, climate-controlled by HVAC (the "Gymnasium"), located at the ICN Bellevue campus; and

WHEREAS, NIA wishes to use the Gymnasium for its educational and physical-education programming on weekdays; and

WHEREAS, ICN Bellevue is willing to grant NIA a non-exclusive license to use the Gymnasium on the terms set out below, including a fair sharing of the operating costs associated with NIA's use;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Gymnasium"** means the approximately 10,000 sq. ft. multi-purpose gym floor, bleachers, and directly associated ancillary spaces (entry lobby, restrooms, and wudu area where applicable) as further described in the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16).
- **"NIA Hours"** means Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, excluding ICN Bellevue-designated holidays and closures under Section 19.
- **"Student"** means any minor or adult enrolled in or attending an NIA program using the Gymnasium under this Agreement.
- **"Facility Manager"** means the ICN Bellevue staff member responsible for day-to-day gymnasium operations.

## 3. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of one (1) academic year, renewing automatically for successive one-year terms unless either Party gives at least ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term. Either Party may propose revised fees or terms for a renewal term at least sixty (60) days before renewal; if the Parties do not agree on revised terms, either Party may decline to renew.

## 4. Grant of Use, Scheduling & Priority

ICN Bellevue grants NIA a non-exclusive license to use the Gymnasium during NIA Hours for physical education, athletics, assemblies, and related school programming, subject to the priority-of-use framework in the Capacity & Crowd Management Policy (ICN-02) and Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16).

- NIA Hours are reserved exclusively for NIA; ICN Bellevue will not book public rentals or community programs during this window.
- Congregational prayer needs take precedence over any activity, including during NIA Hours, if a scheduling conflict arises (e.g., a shortened school day or special prayer observance); ICN Bellevue will give NIA reasonable advance notice of any such conflict.
- A cleaning/turnover buffer of one (1) hour (4:00 PM–5:00 PM) follows NIA Hours before the space reopens to other users.
- Any NIA use outside NIA Hours (e.g., an evening event) requires a separate booking under the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16) and is not covered by this Agreement's cost-sharing terms.

## 5. Facility Use Fee & Cost-Sharing Payment

The Parties agree that NIA will reimburse ICN Bellevue monthly for NIA's proportional share of the Gymnasium's utility, custodial, and routine maintenance costs, calculated as follows.

### 5.1 Estimated Monthly Cost-Sharing Calculation (Year 1 Planning Estimate)

| Cost Category | Facility-Wide Est. (Annual) | Facility-Wide Est. (Monthly) | Allocation Basis | NIA Share % | NIA Monthly Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity (HVAC + lighting) | $18,000 | $1,500 | Usage-hours | 53% | $795 |
| Water & Sewer | $1,500 | $125 | Usage-hours | 53% | $66 |
| Custodial / Janitorial | $6,000 | $500 | Usage-days (5x/week) | 65% | $325 |
| Routine Maintenance Reserve | $8,000 | $667 | Usage-hours | 53% | $354 |
| **TOTAL** | **$33,500** | **$2,792** | | | **~$1,540** |

**Notes on this table:**
- Figures above are planning estimates based on typical commercial gymnasium energy and operating benchmarks for a 10,000 sq. ft. HVAC-conditioned space (approx. $1.80/sq.ft./yr electricity, $0.15/sq.ft./yr water/sewer, $0.60/sq.ft./yr custodial, $0.80/sq.ft./yr routine maintenance reserve) — not yet drawn from ICN Bellevue's actual utility invoices.
- NIA's usage-hours share (53%) is calculated as NIA's 40 hours/week of use divided by an estimated 75 hours/week of total active Gymnasium use across all ICN Bellevue programs, community use, and rentals. This ratio is reviewed annually under Section 6.
- Custodial cost is allocated at a higher 65% share to reflect that NIA's daily (5x/week) use drives the majority of routine cleaning frequency.
- The resulting estimated NIA monthly payment is approximately $1,540, which the Parties may round to a flat $1,550/month for administrative simplicity, subject to the true-up in Section 6.

### 5.2 Payment Terms

- NIA pays the agreed monthly amount in advance, by the 1st of each month, for each month school is in session per the NIA academic calendar.
- No fee applies for months when NIA does not use the Gymnasium (e.g., summer/winter break), unless the Parties agree otherwise.
- Late payment beyond fifteen (15) days accrues interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by Tennessee law, whichever is lower.

## 6. Utilities & Operating Cost Reconciliation (True-Up)

Because Section 5.1 uses planning estimates, the Parties will reconcile estimated costs against actual costs no less than annually:

- ICN Bellevue will provide NIA, within 60 days of each academic year's end, a summary of actual utility invoices, custodial payroll/contract costs, and maintenance expenses for the Gymnasium during the term.
- NIA's actual cost share will be recalculated using the same allocation percentages (or updated percentages if actual usage hours changed materially) applied to actual costs.
- If NIA overpaid relative to actual costs, ICN Bellevue will credit the difference against the next term's payments (or refund it if the Agreement is not renewed). If NIA underpaid, NIA will pay the shortfall within 30 days of receiving the reconciliation statement.
- Either Party may request that ICN Bellevue install a submeter for the Gymnasium's electricity at NIA's cost, to replace the estimated allocation with direct measurement.

## 7. Maintenance & Repair Responsibilities

### 7.1 ICN Bellevue Responsibilities
- Capital systems and structural maintenance: roof, HVAC system replacement/major servicing, plumbing and electrical infrastructure, fire/life-safety systems.
- Routine preventive maintenance per the Facility Maintenance & Housekeeping Policy (ICN-06): HVAC filter changes, pest control, general building upkeep not caused by NIA use.
- Daily/weekly custodial cleaning of the Gymnasium and shared restrooms (cost shared per Section 5).

### 7.2 NIA Responsibilities
- The cost of repairing or replacing any Gymnasium fixture, equipment, flooring, wall, door, or window damaged by an NIA student, staff member, or visitor during NIA Hours, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
- Immediate notification to the Facility Manager of any damage, safety hazard, or equipment malfunction discovered during NIA Hours.
- Ensuring NIA staff and students follow the Gymnasium Usage Policy (ICN-17), including footwear, equipment-use, and floor-care rules.

### 7.3 48-Hour Repair Standard

Any damage to the Gymnasium caused by an NIA student ("Student-Caused Damage") will be repaired within forty-eight (48) hours of the Facility Manager receiving notice, subject to parts/vendor availability beyond ICN Bellevue's reasonable control. Specifically:

- NIA reports Student-Caused Damage to the Facility Manager the same day it occurs or is discovered.
- ICN Bellevue arranges the repair using an approved vendor per the Facility Maintenance & Housekeeping Policy (ICN-06) and completes the repair, or installs a safe temporary fix, within 48 hours of notice.
- ICN Bellevue invoices NIA for the reasonable cost of Student-Caused Damage repairs within 15 days of completion; NIA pays within 30 days of invoice.
- If a repair cannot reasonably be completed within 48 hours (e.g., a specialty part is on backorder), ICN Bellevue will notify NIA of the revised timeline and any interim safety measures (e.g., restricting the damaged area) within the original 48-hour window.
- If Student-Caused Damage makes any portion of the Gymnasium unsafe for use, that portion is restricted from use until repaired, regardless of the 48-hour target.

## 8. Supervision Requirement

NIA is solely responsible for providing adequate, qualified adult supervision of all students at all times students are present in or entering/exiting the Gymnasium during NIA Hours.

- NIA will maintain a minimum adult-to-student supervision ratio appropriate to student age, consistent with Tennessee Department of Education and state licensing guidance for physical education/athletic activities, and will provide ICN Bellevue its supervision-ratio policy upon request.
- At least one NIA supervisor present at any time students are using the Gymnasium must hold current First Aid/CPR/AED certification.
- ICN Bellevue's Facility Manager or staff monitor may be present for facility-operations purposes but is not responsible for student supervision, discipline, or safety monitoring — that responsibility rests entirely with NIA staff.
- NIA supervisory staff working in or around the Gymnasium with students are subject to the background-check expectations in ICN Bellevue's Children, Youth & Family Safety Policy (ICN-09); NIA represents that its staff have passed a criminal background check consistent with Tennessee school-employment requirements.

## 9. Liability, Assumption of Risk & Indemnification

### 9.1 No Liability for Student Injury During NIA Hours
ICN Bellevue is not liable for any injury, illness, or harm to any NIA student, staff member, or visitor arising out of or occurring during NIA's use of the Gymnasium during NIA Hours, except to the extent such injury is directly caused by ICN Bellevue's gross negligence or willful misconduct (e.g., a failure to maintain a structural or life-safety system that ICN Bellevue is responsible for under Section 7.1, of which ICN Bellevue had notice and failed to reasonably address).

### 9.2 Assumption of Risk
NIA acknowledges that physical activity in a gymnasium carries an inherent risk of injury, and NIA assumes that risk on behalf of its program during NIA Hours. NIA is responsible for obtaining any waivers, parental consents, or assumption-of-risk acknowledgments it determines necessary from students/families under its own policies and applicable law.

### 9.3 Indemnification
NIA will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless ICN Bellevue, its Trustees, Executive Committee (EC), staff, and volunteers from and against any claims, damages, losses, and reasonable expenses (including attorney's fees) arising out of: (a) any injury to an NIA student, staff member, or visitor during NIA Hours, except as excluded under Section 9.1; (b) any negligent or wrongful act of NIA or its staff, students, or visitors; or (c) NIA's breach of this Agreement. ICN Bellevue will similarly indemnify NIA for claims arising from ICN Bellevue's gross negligence or willful misconduct in areas of the Gymnasium under ICN Bellevue's direct control and responsibility.

## 10. Insurance Requirements

NIA will maintain, throughout the Term, and provide ICN Bellevue a Certificate of Insurance evidencing:

- Commercial General Liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate, naming "Islamic Center of Nashville at Bellevue Mosque" as an additional insured for activities under this Agreement.
- Student accident/medical insurance or equivalent coverage for NIA students participating in Gymnasium activities, consistent with NIA's standard school insurance program.
- Workers' compensation insurance covering NIA staff, as required by Tennessee law.

ICN Bellevue will maintain its own general liability, property, and umbrella insurance for the Gymnasium consistent with the Insurance & Legal Compliance Policy (ICN-15).

## 11. Access, Security & Keys

- ICN Bellevue staff will open and secure the Gymnasium for NIA Hours; NIA is not issued building keys or alarm codes, consistent with the Security Policy (ICN-05).
- NIA will notify the Facility Manager promptly of any security concern, unauthorized visitor, or safety incident occurring during NIA Hours.
- NIA's use is limited to the Gymnasium and directly associated ancillary spaces identified in Section 2; NIA staff/students may not enter prayer halls, offices, or mechanical rooms without ICN Bellevue staff escort.

## 12. Condition of Premises & Inspections

- ICN Bellevue will deliver the Gymnasium in clean, safe, working condition at the start of each term.
- The Facility Manager and an NIA representative will conduct a joint walkthrough at the start and end of each academic year (and may do so periodically during the year) to document the Gymnasium's condition and note any existing damage.
- ICN Bellevue may inspect the Gymnasium at reasonable times to confirm compliance with this Agreement and applicable policies.

## 13. Compliance with ICN Bellevue Policies

NIA and its staff/students will comply with all applicable ICN Bellevue facility policies while using the Gymnasium, including but not limited to the Gymnasium Usage Policy (ICN-17), Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16), Health, Safety & Emergency Management Policy (ICN-04), Children, Youth & Family Safety Policy (ICN-09), and Community Code of Conduct Policy (ICN-14), each as may be updated by ICN Bellevue from time to time with reasonable notice to NIA.

## 14. Prohibited Uses & Conduct

- No alcohol, illegal drugs, smoking/vaping, weapons, or gambling anywhere on ICN Bellevue property, at any time.
- No activity, imagery, or conduct inconsistent with the values of an Islamic center, consistent with the Community Code of Conduct Policy (ICN-14).
- No use of the Gymnasium for purposes outside NIA's educational programming without ICN Bellevue's prior written consent (e.g., NIA may not sublet or allow a third party to use the space).

## 15. Alterations; No Subletting

NIA will not make any structural or permanent alteration to the Gymnasium without ICN Bellevue's prior written consent, and will not sublet, assign, or allow any third party to use the Gymnasium under this Agreement without ICN Bellevue's prior written consent.

## 16. Emergency Procedures

NIA staff will familiarize themselves with, and follow, ICN Bellevue's Emergency Action Plan (per ICN-04) while in the Gymnasium, including evacuation routes, severe-weather shelter locations, and AED/first-aid locations. In any life-threatening emergency, NIA staff will call 911 first and notify ICN Bellevue staff as soon as practicable.

## 17. Default & Termination

- Either Party may terminate this Agreement for the other's material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice describing the breach.
- ICN Bellevue may suspend or terminate NIA's access immediately, without the standard cure period, for an imminent safety threat, repeated serious violations of Section 14, or non-payment continuing more than sixty (60) days past due.
- Upon termination or expiration, NIA will vacate the Gymnasium, remove any NIA-owned property, and pay any outstanding amounts owed within 15 days.

## 18. Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for failure to perform due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, public health orders, fire, or utility failure. If ICN Bellevue must close the Gymnasium for such reasons during NIA Hours, the monthly fee will be prorated for the affected days, and the Parties will work in good faith on makeup scheduling where feasible.

## 19. Independent Status; No Agency

NIA is not an agent, partner, or joint venturer of ICN Bellevue, and this Agreement does not create an employment relationship between ICN Bellevue and any NIA staff or student. Each Party is responsible for its own staff, students, and operations.

## 20. Confidentiality & Data

Each Party will keep confidential any student, staff, or financial information shared by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, and will not disclose such information to third parties except as required by law or with the disclosing Party's consent.

## 21. Dispute Resolution; Governing Law

The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through good-faith negotiation between the Executive Committee (EC) and NIA's designated administrator. If unresolved within 30 days, the Parties will attempt mediation before pursuing other remedies. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles, and venue for any legal proceeding will lie in Davidson County, Tennessee.

## 22. Notices

All notices under this Agreement will be in writing and delivered by email (with read confirmation) or certified mail to each Party's designated contact, as updated from time to time by written notice.

## 23. Entire Agreement; Amendment; Severability; Assignment

- This Agreement, including any referenced ICN Bellevue policies, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding NIA's use of the Gymnasium and supersedes any prior discussions or understandings on this subject.
- This Agreement may be amended only in writing, signed by both Parties.
- If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
- Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except that ICN Bellevue may assign it to a successor entity in the event of a reorganization.
