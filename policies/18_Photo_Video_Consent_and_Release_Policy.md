## 1. Purpose
To protect the privacy and dignity of congregants — especially children and women — by setting clear rules for when photos or video may be taken at ICN Bellevue, how consent is obtained, and how images and recordings may be used.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all photography and videography taken on ICN Bellevue property by staff, volunteers, hired photographers/videographers, renters, media, and individual congregants, across all spaces including the prayer halls, gymnasium, classrooms, and grounds.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 General Principle
Photography and video are permitted at ICN Bellevue for legitimate community, educational, and promotional purposes, but never at the expense of an individual's reasonable expectation of privacy — particularly in the women's prayer section, changing/locker areas, restrooms, and wudu areas, where photography and recording are prohibited at all times.

### 3.2 Consent Requirements
- **Official ICN Bellevue photography/video** (for the website, social media, newsletters, annual reports): a general blanket notice is posted at entrances and on event registration forms stating that photography may occur; attendees who do not wish to be photographed may notify staff and will be reasonably accommodated (e.g., seated outside camera framing).
- **Identifiable, featured use** of an individual's image or a named quote (e.g., a profile story, a testimonial, a large-format photo) requires a signed Photo/Video Release Form from the individual (or parent/guardian if a minor) before publication.
- **Minors**: no photo or video identifying a specific child by name, or any close-up/featured image of a child, is published without a signed parental/guardian consent form on file. General wide-shots of a group activity (e.g., a classroom from the back of the room) fall under the blanket notice in §3.2, not the individual release requirement — but any doubt is resolved in favor of requiring consent.
- **Livestreamed khutbahs/events**: covered under the IT, AV & Communications Policy (ICN-13); camera framing is planned to reasonably minimize close-up capture of the congregation, particularly the women's section.

### 3.3 Consent Withdrawal
- Any individual (or parent/guardian of a minor) may request that a previously published photo or video be removed. Requests are honored within a reasonable timeframe (target: 10 business days) for content ICN Bellevue controls directly (website, social media accounts).

### 3.4 Outside Photographers, Renters & Media
- Renters hosting private events (weddings, receptions) may bring their own photographer/videographer for their own event coverage; that footage is the renter's property and is not subject to this policy, but the photographer must still respect the prohibited areas in §3.1 and any request from other congregants present (e.g., in shared spaces) not to be filmed.
- Press or outside media requesting to film or photograph at ICN Bellevue must be approved in advance by the Executive Committee (EC) or designee and are escorted by staff at all times, per the Media & Public Statement Policy (ICN-19).

### 3.5 Storage & Use of Consent Forms
- Signed release forms are filed (physically or digitally) and retained for as long as the associated image/video may reasonably be in use, consistent with the Document Retention Policy.
- Consent forms specify the intended use (website, social media, print materials, etc.); use beyond what was consented to requires new consent.

## 4. Procedures
1. Event registration and program enrollment forms include the blanket photography notice and an opt-out field.
2. Any staff/volunteer planning a featured photo, video, or story involving an identifiable individual obtains a signed release before the content is used.
3. Youth program enrollment includes a parental photo/video consent checkbox, tracked alongside other program paperwork.
4. Consent withdrawal requests are logged and routed to the IT/Communications Coordinator for removal from ICN Bellevue-controlled platforms.
5. Outside media requests are routed to the Executive Committee (EC) for approval before any filming begins.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **IT/Communications Coordinator**: Maintains release forms, manages consent withdrawal requests, ensures published content complies with consent scope.
- **Program/Event Staff**: Collect signed releases before featuring an individual; enforce no-photography zones.
- **Executive Committee (EC)**: Approves outside media/press filming requests.
