## 1. Purpose
To protect the life and safety of all congregants, staff, and visitors through fire safety systems, emergency planning, medical response readiness, and accessibility compliance.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the entire ICN Bellevue campus, all staff, volunteers, and congregants, and all scheduled and unscheduled gatherings.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Fire Safety
- Fire extinguishers inspected and tagged at least annually by a licensed contractor; visually checked monthly by facility staff.
- Fire alarm and sprinkler systems (if installed) tested per the schedule required by the local Fire Marshal.
- Exit signage illuminated and visible at all times the building is occupied; evacuation maps posted in each major room.

### 3.2 Emergency Action Plan (EAP)
ICN Bellevue maintains a written EAP covering:
- Fire
- Active threat / lockdown
- Medical emergency
- Severe weather (tornado, extreme heat/cold as applicable to Middle Tennessee)
- Bomb threat / suspicious package
- Gas leak / utility failure

The EAP is posted in staff areas and reviewed with all staff and key volunteers annually.

### 3.3 Drills
- A minimum of one full evacuation drill is conducted per year, with at least one drill scheduled during a high-attendance prayer (e.g., Jummah) to test real-world crowd conditions.
- Drill results are logged and gaps addressed within 30 days.

### 3.4 First Aid / Medical Response
- At least one Automated External Defibrillator (AED) is maintained on-site, inspected monthly, with battery/pad expiration tracked.
- A minimum number of staff/volunteers (set by the Safety Committee) hold current CPR/First Aid certification, with priority coverage during Jummah, Taraweeh, and Eid.
- First aid kits stocked and located in each wing of the building; contents checked quarterly.
- A designated medical response team is identified for peak prayers, with a clear 911 call protocol and an incident log for every medical event.

### 3.5 Accessibility (ADA/Accessibility Compliance)
- Ramps, accessible wudu stations, accessible restrooms, and wheelchair seating are provided and kept clear of obstruction.
- Accessible parking spaces are maintained near the main entrance per local code.

### 3.6 Incident Reporting
- Any injury, near-miss, or safety-relevant event is logged on an Incident Report Form within 24 hours and reviewed monthly by the Safety/Facilities Committee.

### 3.7 Weather Closure Policy
- Closure or delay decisions (severe storms, tornado warnings, extreme heat/cold advisories) are made by the Executive Committee (EC) or designee, communicated via the mass notification system, and posted on the website/social media.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager schedules and logs all fire-system inspections and AED/first-aid checks.
2. Safety Committee schedules the annual drill and documents attendance and outcomes.
3. Any incident triggers completion of the Incident Report Form, reviewed at the next Safety Committee meeting.
4. EAP reviewed and re-briefed to staff annually, or immediately after any real incident.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Owns fire-system compliance, AED/first-aid readiness, incident log.
- **Safety Committee**: Runs annual drills, reviews incident reports, updates the EAP.
- **All Staff/Key Volunteers**: Know their role in the EAP and complete required safety training.
