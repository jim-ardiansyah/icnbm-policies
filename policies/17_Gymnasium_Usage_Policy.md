> This policy sets the day-to-day rules for anyone using the gymnasium — members, drop-in users, youth programs, and event attendees. It applies in addition to, not instead of, the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16), which governs bookings, pricing, and rental administration.

---

## 1. Purpose
To keep the gymnasium safe, clean, and usable for everyone by setting clear, consistently enforced rules of use for all individuals in the space, regardless of whether they are there for a personal workout, a scheduled program, or a booked event.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the gymnasium floor, bleachers, and directly associated ancillary spaces (lobby, restrooms, wudu area) whenever any individual is using them — including ICN Bellevue members, NIA students during school hours, youth program participants, drop-in users during open hours, and attendees of a booked rental.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Access & Hours
- The gym is only open for use during posted hours (see the current weekly schedule posted at the entrance and in the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy, §3.2).
- Users may only be present during their scheduled program, booking, or posted open-use window — not before or after.
- A Facility Manager or trained monitor must be present whenever the gym is in use; do not use the gym if no staff/monitor is on-site.
- Entry is through designated doors only. Do not prop open exterior doors or admit others without staff knowledge.

### 3.2 Footwear & Floor Care
- Non-marking, clean athletic shoes only on the playing floor. No street shoes, cleats, sandals, or bare feet during athletic activity (bare feet permitted only where separately allowed, e.g., wudu area).
- No dragging equipment, chairs, or tables across the floor — lift and carry, or use protective floor covering where provided.
- No food, gum, or drinks other than capped water bottles on the playing floor.
- Report any spill, damaged flooring, or slip hazard to staff immediately.

### 3.3 Permitted Activities
- Basketball, volleyball, futsal/indoor soccer, badminton, and similar court sports.
- Youth programs, practices, clinics, and supervised recreational activity.
- Any additional activity type included in an approved booking (see ICN-16).

### 3.4 Prohibited Conduct & Items
- Alcohol, illegal drugs, and smoking/vaping (including hookah) — anywhere on the property, at all times.
- Weapons of any kind, except sworn law enforcement or licensed security engaged for an event.
- Gambling or games of chance for money.
- Rough play, fighting, or any activity that endangers other users.
- Profanity, harassment, or disrespectful language or behavior toward other users, staff, or volunteers.
- Climbing on bleachers, hoops, nets, stage equipment, or railings.
- Bringing in personal sports equipment (e.g., weighted balls, personal nets) without staff approval.
- Photography or recording of other users without their consent, particularly in the women's-use time slots or family areas.

### 3.5 Equipment Use
- Gym equipment (balls, nets, hoops, bleachers) is available for use as provided by staff; users do not operate retractable bleachers or basketball hoop mechanisms — staff only.
- Equipment is returned to its designated storage location after use; do not leave equipment on the floor or block exits with it.
- Report damaged or missing equipment to staff before leaving.

### 3.6 Supervision of Minors
- Children and youth must be directly supervised by a parent, guardian, or authorized program staff member at all times; the gym is not a drop-off childcare space.
- During youth programs, supervision ratios follow the Children, Youth & Family Safety Policy (ICN-09).
- Unaccompanied minors will be asked to wait with staff until a supervising adult arrives.

### 3.7 Prayer Time Accommodation
- All gym activity pauses briefly and quietly for congregational prayer when it coincides with use, consistent with the Center's mission.
- Users needing to step out for wudu or prayer during their session are accommodated without loss of their scheduled time.

### 3.8 Dress & Conduct Expectations
- Athletic wear is expected to be modest and appropriate for a house-of-worship setting — no offensive graphics, language, or attire inconsistent with Center values.
- Users are expected to treat the space, staff, and one another with respect at all times.

### 3.9 Injuries & Incidents
- Any injury, no matter how minor, is reported to the staff/monitor on duty immediately.
- Staff will provide first aid, use the AED if needed, and call 911 for any serious injury.
- All incidents are logged on the Incident Report Form per the Health, Safety & Emergency Management Policy (ICN-04).

### 3.10 Guests & Visitors
- Members and program participants may bring guests only during open-use hours and must remain responsible for their guest's conduct.
- Guests follow the same rules as members; staff may ask a disruptive guest to leave.

## 4. Procedures
1. Users check in with the staff/monitor on duty upon arrival (sign-in sheet or program roster, as applicable).
2. Staff briefly confirms footwear, activity type, and any equipment needed before use begins.
3. Staff monitors the floor throughout the session and intervenes for any rule violation, starting with a verbal reminder.
4. Repeated or serious violations are documented and escalated per §5 below.
5. At the end of use, staff confirms equipment is returned and the floor is left clear before the next group or closing.

## 5. Enforcement
- **First violation** (minor, e.g., wrong footwear, food on the floor): verbal reminder and correction.
- **Repeated or moderate violation** (e.g., disruptive conduct, unauthorized equipment use): written warning logged with the Facility Manager; may result in the user being asked to leave for the day.
- **Serious violation** (e.g., prohibited substances, weapons, harassment, violence): immediate removal from the facility, incident documented, and possible suspension of gym privileges pending Facilities Committee review.
- Suspension or ban decisions may be appealed in writing to the Facilities Manager within 10 business days, following the appeals process in the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16, §3.16).

## 6. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager / Monitor on Duty**: Enforces this policy in real time, logs incidents, and manages check-in.
- **Program Staff/Coaches**: Responsible for supervision ratios and conduct of their participants during their session.
- **Users**: Responsible for following posted rules, reporting hazards or injuries, and treating the space and others with respect.
