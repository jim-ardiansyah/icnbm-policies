## 1. Purpose
To protect congregants, staff, and property through access control, surveillance, trained personnel, and coordinated emergency response with local law enforcement.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the entire ICN Bellevue campus, including parking areas, all staff, volunteers, security personnel, and visitors.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Access Control
- Staff-only areas (offices, mechanical rooms, storage) are secured with keyed or fob access.
- A master key/fob log is maintained by the Facility Manager, listing every holder and access level.
- After-hours entry requires a logged reason and, where possible, two-person access.

### 3.2 Video Surveillance (CCTV)
- Cameras cover all entrances, exits, parking areas, and main hallways.
- Footage is retained for a minimum of 30 days (up to 90 days where storage allows).
- Footage may be reviewed only by the Executive Committee (EC), Security Committee Chair, or law enforcement upon lawful request; all reviews are logged.

### 3.3 Security Personnel & Volunteers
- Trained greeters/ushers are stationed at every major entrance during Jummah, Taraweeh, and Eid, wearing visible ID badges or vests.
- A volunteer security team is recruited, trained, and scheduled by the Security Committee, with background checks required for all members.

### 3.4 Active Threat Preparedness
- Staff and key volunteers receive Run-Hide-Fight (or equivalent) training at least annually.
- ICN Bellevue maintains a direct liaison relationship with the local police department (Metro Nashville Police Department / Bellevue precinct), including periodic facility walkthroughs and a shared emergency contact list.
- A silent alarm or rapid-notification method (e.g., panic button, group text alert) is established for front-desk and office staff.

### 3.5 Lost Child / Missing Person Protocol
- A designated code phrase and reunification point are established for use during high-attendance events.
- Any report of a missing child triggers immediate notification of security staff, building lockdown of exits if warranted, and police notification if the child is not located within a defined short window (e.g., 10 minutes).

### 3.6 Visitor & Interfaith Tour Policy
- Visitors and tour groups sign in at the front desk during non-prayer hours and are escorted by a designated staff member or volunteer.
- School and interfaith group visits are scheduled in advance with the Outreach/Events Coordinator.

### 3.7 Cybersecurity
- Donor and membership databases, website, and livestream account credentials are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication where available.
- Access to financial and donor systems is limited to authorized finance/admin staff.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager issues and logs all keys/fobs; conducts an annual access audit.
2. Security Committee schedules greeter/usher coverage for every Jummah, Taraweeh night, and Eid.
3. Any security incident (suspicious activity, threat, unauthorized access) is documented on an Incident Report Form and reviewed within 48 hours.
4. Annual active-threat training scheduled and attendance logged.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Security Committee Chair**: Oversees volunteer security team, CCTV access log, police liaison relationship.
- **Facility Manager**: Manages access control hardware and key/fob log.
- **Front Desk/Office Staff**: Manage visitor sign-in and use rapid-notification tools when needed.
