## 1. Purpose
To ensure the safety congregants by preventing overcrowding, maintaining clear egress paths, and providing a documented plan for peak-attendance events (Jummah, Taraweeh, Eid).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all prayer halls, overflow spaces, entrances/exits, and outdoor areas used during any masjid gathering.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Posted Occupancy Limits
- Each room used for gathering (main hall, overflow hall, gymnasium, basement) must have its maximum occupancy set by the local Fire Marshal/Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) and posted visibly at each entrance.
- **Posted limits are never to be exceeded**, including during Jummah, Taraweeh, or Eid, regardless of community demand.

### 3.2 Overflow Planning
- A designated secondary prayer space (e.g., gymnasium, multipurpose hall, or tented outdoor area) shall be pre-approved and equipped with an audio/video relay of the main hall khutbah and prayer.
- Overflow activation is triggered automatically once the main hall reaches 90% of posted capacity — not after it is already full.

### 3.3 Jummah (Friday) Protocol
- Volunteer crowd marshals stationed at each entrance to monitor and (if needed) count attendees in/out.
- One-way flow for entry and exit where the building layout allows.
- Shoe-storage racks positioned so they never block aisles, doorways, or marked exits.

### 3.4 Ramadan / Taraweeh & Eid Surge Plan
- A written, dated surge plan is prepared annually before Ramadan, covering: staffing levels, overflow activation, parking/traffic routing, and (for Eid) the option of multiple prayer shifts/timings to split total attendance across the morning.
- The plan is reviewed with all volunteers/marshals at a mandatory briefing before the season begins.

### 3.5 Turn-Away Protocol
- Once a hall reaches posted capacity, staff direct arriving congregants to the overflow space or a livestream/relay room.
- No congregant is permitted to stand in aisles, stairwells, or in front of exit doors as a substitute for turning away.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager confirms current posted occupancy limits with the Fire Marshal annually or after any renovation.
2. Head usher/marshal monitors hall fill-level during high-attendance prayers using a simple clicker count or headcount estimate.
3. At 90% capacity, marshal radios/signals staff to open and direct traffic to overflow space.
4. After each Jummah/Eid, marshals debrief with the Facility Manager to note bottlenecks for the next event.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Confirms occupancy limits, maintains overflow-space readiness, leads annual surge-plan update.
- **Volunteer Crowd Marshals**: Monitor entrances/exits, redirect overflow, keep aisles clear.
- **Security Team**: Supports crowd flow at Eid and other high-attendance events.
