## 1. Purpose
To protect the safety and wellbeing of children participating in ICN Bellevue programs (weekend school, youth groups, childcare during prayer).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all youth program staff, volunteers, and any area of the facility used for children's activities.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Two-Adult Rule
- No staff member or volunteer is ever alone with a child. Classrooms and activity areas use windowed or open doors at all times.

### 3.2 Background Checks
- All staff and volunteers working with children pass a criminal background check before their first unsupervised interaction with minors, renewed per the HR Staff & Volunteer Policy.

### 3.3 Check-In / Check-Out
- Children's programs (weekend school, prayer-time childcare) use a sign-in/sign-out system.
- Children are released only to a parent/guardian or an individual on the authorized pickup list provided at registration.

### 3.4 Supervision Ratios
- Minimum adult-to-child ratios are maintained based on age group (e.g., approximately 1:10–1:15 for school-age children, higher ratio for younger children), set annually by the Youth Committee.

### 3.5 Playground & Recreational Space Safety
- Any playground or gym space is inspected regularly for hazards, with equipment maintenance logged.
- Recreational activities are supervised at all times by a background-checked adult.

### 3.6 Mandatory Reporting
- All youth-facing staff and volunteers complete mandatory-reporting training and understand their legal obligation to report suspected child abuse or neglect to the appropriate authorities.

## 4. Procedures
1. Youth Director confirms background-check status before assigning any volunteer to a children's program.
2. Sign-in/sign-out sheets and authorized pickup lists maintained for every session.
3. Supervision ratios checked and logged at the start of each program.
4. Any safety concern or incident involving a child is documented immediately and reported to the Executive Committee (EC) and, where required by law, to the appropriate authorities.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Youth Director**: Owns program safety compliance, ratios, and check-in/out system.
- **HR Committee**: Verifies background checks and training completion.
- **All Youth Program Volunteers**: Follow the Two-Adult Rule and report concerns immediately.
