## 1. Purpose
To manage utility costs responsibly and reduce the environmental footprint of the ICN Bellevue facility while maintaining a comfortable environment for worship.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all HVAC, lighting, water, and waste systems on the ICN Bellevue campus.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Energy Management
- Programmable/smart thermostats used to reduce HVAC run-time during unoccupied hours.
- LED lighting used throughout the facility, with motion-sensor lighting in low-traffic areas (offices, storage, restrooms).
- HVAC set-point policy balances comfort for large Jummah/Eid crowds against energy cost during off-peak hours.

### 3.2 Water Conservation
- Low-flow fixtures installed at all wudu stations and restrooms.
- Leak-check inspection performed quarterly; any leak repaired within 48 hours of discovery.

### 3.3 Waste Management
- Standard recycling stream maintained for paper, cardboard, and plastics.
- Waste pickup frequency increased during Ramadan and Eid to handle higher volume from Iftar/event activity.

### 3.4 Utility Cost Monitoring
- Facility Manager reviews utility bills monthly against budget and flags any unexplained spike for investigation.
- Annual utility spend reviewed by the Finance Committee as part of budget planning.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager sets seasonal thermostat schedules and adjusts for major events.
2. Quarterly leak-check and utility review logged in the maintenance calendar.
3. Waste hauler contract reviewed annually, with temporary added pickups scheduled ahead of Ramadan.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Owns thermostat scheduling, leak checks, and utility monitoring.
- **Finance Committee**: Reviews annual utility spend as part of budget approval.
