## 1. Purpose
To manage parking capacity and traffic flow safely during regular prayers and high-attendance events, and to maintain good relations with neighbors and local traffic authorities.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the ICN Bellevue parking lot, any overflow arrangements, and surrounding streets used by congregants.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Parking Capacity Plan
- On-site parking capacity is documented; when expected attendance (e.g., Jummah, Eid) exceeds on-site capacity, an overflow plan is activated (agreements with nearby lots, shuttle service, or coordinated street parking where legally permitted).

### 3.2 Traffic Control
- Volunteer traffic marshals, wearing high-visibility vests, direct vehicle flow during Eid and other high-attendance events.
- For events with expected significant traffic impact, the Facility Manager coordinates with the Metro Nashville traffic/police department in advance for any needed permits or officer support.

### 3.3 Accessible Parking
- Accessible parking spaces are maintained near the main entrance in the number required by local code, kept clear at all times.

### 3.4 Neighbor Relations
- Congregants are asked not to block neighboring driveways or private property; any complaint from neighbors is logged and addressed promptly by the Facility Manager.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager reviews on-site parking capacity annually and updates the overflow plan before Ramadan.
2. Traffic marshals briefed before every Eid prayer on assigned positions and signals.
3. Any neighbor complaint logged and followed up within 48 hours.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Owns the parking/overflow plan and neighbor-complaint log.
- **Security Committee**: Recruits and trains traffic marshals for peak events.
