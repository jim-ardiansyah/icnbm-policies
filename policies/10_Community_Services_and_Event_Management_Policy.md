## 1. Purpose
To provide a consistent, fair process for booking the facility for weddings, funerals, community events, and food service, while protecting the building and managing liability.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all internal and external bookings of ICN Bellevue halls, kitchen, and grounds.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Facility Rental / Booking
- A published fee schedule applies to hall rentals for weddings, community events, and other bookings; members may receive discounted rates as set by the Board.
- A security deposit is required for all outside bookings, refunded after a post-event walkthrough confirms no damage.
- Outside renters must provide a Certificate of Insurance naming ICN Bellevue as additionally insured for events above a threshold size set by the Board.

### 3.2 Funeral (Janazah) Services
- Handled per the Prayer & Worship Operations Policy; no rental fee applies to janazah services for the community.

### 3.3 Food Service
- Any food service or sale on-site complies with applicable health department permitting requirements.
- Community potlucks and Iftars follow basic food-safety guidance: hot food kept above 140°F, cold food below 40°F, and any homemade dish should list major allergens where practical.
- Kitchen use by outside groups requires prior approval and a walkthrough of kitchen rules.

### 3.4 Ramadan Iftar Program
- A written annual Iftar plan covers staffing, crowd flow into the dining/hall area, cleanup schedule, and the process for accepting daily Iftar sponsorships/donations.
- Sponsor names and dates are tracked by the Events Coordinator to avoid double-booking a night.

## 4. Procedures
1. Booking requests submitted to the Events Coordinator with date, purpose, and expected attendance.
2. Coordinator checks the calendar for conflicts with prayer times and existing bookings, then confirms with a signed rental agreement and deposit.
3. Post-event walkthrough conducted within 48 hours; deposit refunded if no damage found.
4. Ramadan Iftar sponsorship calendar published at least one month before Ramadan begins.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Events Coordinator**: Manages the booking calendar, contracts, deposits, and Iftar sponsorship schedule.
- **Facility Manager**: Conducts pre- and post-event walkthroughs.
- **Kitchen Volunteers**: Follow food-safety guidance for all community meal service.
