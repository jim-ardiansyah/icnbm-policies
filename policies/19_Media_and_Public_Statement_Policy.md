## 1. Purpose
To ensure that any statement made to the press, on social media, or in public forums on behalf of ICN Bellevue is authorized, accurate, and consistent — protecting the Center's reputation, its nonprofit status, and the community it represents, especially on sensitive or fast-moving topics.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all staff, volunteers, Trustees, and the Imam when speaking or writing in any capacity that could reasonably be understood as representing ICN Bellevue, including interviews, press releases, official social media accounts, op-eds, and public community statements.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Authorized Spokespersons
- The **Executive Committee (EC)** and the **Executive Committee Chair** are the default authorized spokespersons for ICN Bellevue on operational, administrative, and organizational matters.
- The **Imam** is the default authorized spokesperson on religious/theological matters.
- Any other staff, volunteer, or Trustee speaking to media or issuing a public statement on ICN Bellevue's behalf must be specifically designated for that purpose in advance.
- No individual may claim to speak "for the mosque" or "on behalf of ICN Bellevue" without this authorization, even if their intent is well-meaning.

### 3.2 Press & Media Requests
- All media inquiries (reporters, TV crews, podcasts, documentary requests) are directed to the Executive Committee (EC), who determines whether to respond, decline, or refer the request to the Imam or another designated spokesperson.
- On-site filming or interviews by outside media require advance approval and staff escort per the Photo/Video Consent & Release Policy (ICN-18).
- Requests related to a security incident, law enforcement matter, or active legal issue are referred to the Executive Committee (EC) and, where relevant, legal counsel before any response is given.

### 3.3 Social Media & Website
- Only individuals with authorized admin access (per the IT, AV & Communications Policy, ICN-13) may post on ICN Bellevue's official accounts.
- Posts on sensitive current events (geopolitical issues, national controversies, local incidents affecting the Muslim community) are reviewed by the Executive Committee (EC) before posting.
- Staff and volunteers posting on their personal accounts should not present personal opinions as ICN Bellevue's official position, and are encouraged to add a personal-views disclaimer when discussing masjid-related topics publicly.

### 3.4 Political & Advocacy Statements
- As a 501(c)(3) organization, ICN Bellevue does not endorse or oppose political candidates or parties in any official communication.
- Statements on public policy issues (e.g., interfaith relations, civil rights, community safety) may be issued only with Executive Committee (EC) and, for significant statements, Board of Trustees review, to ensure consistency with nonprofit compliance requirements.

### 3.5 Crisis & Emergency Communications
- In the event of a security incident, accident, or other emergency affecting the community, the Executive Committee (EC) or designee is responsible for any public statement, coordinated where appropriate with law enforcement.
- Internal emergency notifications to the congregation (closures, safety alerts) are governed separately by the Health, Safety & Emergency Management Policy (ICN-04) and are not subject to the media-approval steps above, given their time-sensitive nature.

## 4. Procedures
1. Any media contact received by staff or volunteers is forwarded immediately to the Executive Committee (EC), not answered directly unless the individual is a designated spokesperson.
2. For a scheduled interview or press event, the Executive Committee (EC) prepares key messages/talking points in advance, consulting the Imam for any religious content.
3. Sensitive social media posts are drafted and reviewed by the Executive Committee (EC) before publishing, with response time targets set based on urgency.
4. Any public statement involving political or advocacy content is escalated to the Board of Trustees for review before release.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Executive Committee (EC)**: Primary spokesperson for operational matters; reviews and approves sensitive communications; routes media requests.
- **Imam**: Spokesperson for religious/theological matters.
- **IT/Communications Coordinator**: Manages official social media accounts and posting access.
- **Board of Trustees**: Reviews significant public policy statements and any matter with legal or reputational risk.
