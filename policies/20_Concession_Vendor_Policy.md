## 1. Purpose
To set clear rules for any food truck, concession stand, snack sale, or pop-up vendor operating on ICN Bellevue property during tournaments, Ramadan, Eid, or other large events, ensuring food safety, halal compliance, fair vendor access, and protection of the facility. This policy is separate from the kitchen/catering rules for booked private rentals already covered in the Gymnasium Operations & Rental Policy (ICN-16, §3.9) and Community Services & Event Management Policy (ICN-10).

## 2. Scope
Applies to all outside food vendors, concession operators, and pop-up sellers (food, drink, or merchandise) operating on ICN Bellevue grounds, whether invited by ICN Bellevue directly or as part of a renter's event (e.g., a tournament organizer bringing in food trucks).

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Halal & Food Compliance
- All food and beverages sold or served on ICN Bellevue property must be halal; no pork, pork by-products, or alcohol in any ingredient, preparation, or serving.
- Vendors must be able to represent their halal sourcing/preparation practices; where reasonably available, halal-certified vendors are preferred, and the Facility Manager may request documentation.
- Any food vendor preparing or serving food to the public must hold a current health department permit applicable to their operation (e.g., mobile food unit permit, temporary event permit) and provide proof before the event.

### 3.2 Vendor Approval & Booking
- All concessions/vendor participation is approved in advance by the Facility Manager or Events Committee Chair; no walk-up or unapproved vendor may set up on ICN Bellevue property.
- Vendors provide: business name and contact, menu/product list, proof of halal compliance, health permit (if applicable), and a Certificate of Insurance naming ICN Bellevue as additional insured where the vendor's own equipment, cooking, or open flame is involved.
- A vendor fee or revenue-share arrangement (flat fee, percentage of sales, or free participation for community/nonprofit vendors) is set by the Facility Manager within ranges approved by the Board, and confirmed in writing before the event.

### 3.3 Site & Safety Requirements
- Vendor placement (location, spacing, access to power/water if needed) is determined by the Facility Manager to protect fire lanes, accessible routes, and building exits.
- Any open flame, propane, or generator use requires prior approval and, where applicable, a Fire Marshal permit, consistent with the Health, Safety & Emergency Management Policy (ICN-04).
- Vendors are responsible for their own trash and grease disposal; no dumping of grease, food waste, or wastewater into ICN Bellevue drains, wudu areas, or restroom sinks, consistent with the Facility Maintenance & Housekeeping Policy (ICN-06).
- Vendors must vacate and restore their space (no oil stains, debris, or leftover equipment) by the agreed load-out time; damage or excess cleanup is billed similarly to the renter damage-deposit process in ICN-16.

### 3.4 Conduct & Values Alignment
- Vendor products, signage, and staff conduct must be consistent with ICN Bellevue's values (no inappropriate imagery, music, or messaging), per the Community Code of Conduct Policy (ICN-14).
- Vendors and their staff are expected to be respectful of prayer times, pausing sales activity briefly and quietly if a congregational prayer coincides with the event.

### 3.5 Renter-Arranged Vendors
- When a renter (e.g., a tournament organizer) wishes to bring in their own food trucks or vendors, the renter is responsible for ensuring those vendors meet this policy's halal, permit, and insurance requirements, and must submit the vendor list to the Facility Manager for approval at least 14 days before the event.
- ICN Bellevue reserves the right to reject any vendor that does not meet halal, safety, or conduct requirements, even if pre-approved by the renter.

## 4. Procedures
1. Vendor interest submitted to the Facility Manager/Events Committee Chair with menu, halal compliance information, and permit documentation.
2. Facility Manager reviews and approves or declines, confirms placement/site logistics, and sets the fee or revenue-share arrangement in writing.
3. For renter-arranged vendors, the renter submits the vendor list at least 14 days before the event for the same review and approval.
4. Facility Manager or designee inspects vendor setup on event day for compliance (site placement, fire safety, halal signage) before sales begin.
5. Post-event walkthrough confirms the vendor site is clean and undamaged before vendor payment/settlement is finalized.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Approves vendors, sets site placement and safety requirements, conducts day-of and post-event inspection.
- **Events Committee Chair**: Coordinates vendor logistics for larger events and tournaments, tracks fees/revenue-share.
- **Renter (where applicable)**: Ensures any vendor they bring in meets this policy before the event.
- **Vendors**: Maintain halal compliance, health permits, and site cleanliness; carry required insurance.
