## 1. Purpose
- Establish clear, consistent rules for operating and renting the Multi-Purpose Gymnasium.
- Protect the safety of all users and the integrity of ICN Bellevue property.
- Balance the religious mission of the Center, the contractual educational use by Nashville International Academic (NIA), and community/rental demand.
- Generate reasonable, mission-aligned revenue to sustain operations, maintenance, and reserves.

## 2. Scope & Definitions

### 2.1 Scope
This policy applies to the gymnasium floor, bleachers, stage, storage, and directly associated ancillary spaces (lobby, restrooms, wudu area, and warming kitchen when included in a booking). It applies to all users: ICN Bellevue staff and programs, NIA, members, community groups, nonprofits, and commercial renters. It does not override the mosque's religious-use rules or NIA's separate use agreement.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Priority of Use (highest to lowest)
1. ICN Bellevue religious observances and congregational needs (including daily prayer times).
2. NIA weekday program, 8 AM–4 PM Mon–Fri (contractual).
3. ICN Bellevue community, youth, and internal programs.
4. Weekend rentals (prioritized Sat–Sun) and weekday evening rentals.

### 3.2 Operating Hours & Access

**Hard constraints (non-negotiable):**
- Weekdays 8:00 AM–4:00 PM: facility reserved and closed to the public — dedicated to NIA. No public rentals or drop-in use.
- Weekdays 4:00 PM–5:00 PM: cleaning/turnover buffer — closed to the public.
- Weekday public hours begin at 5:00 PM.
- Weekends (Sat–Sun): rental-priority; must be reservable via the online reservation system.
- Alcohol, illegal drugs, and smoking/vaping are prohibited on ICN Bellevue property at all times.

**Standard weekly schedule**

| Day | Block | Designated Use | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon–Fri | 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM | NIA dedicated use | Closed to public; NIA only |
| Mon–Fri | 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM | Cleaning & turnover buffer | Closed; staff only |
| Mon–Fri | 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM | Community programs & public rentals | Open (reservation or program) |
| Saturday | 8:00 AM – 11:00 PM* | Rental-priority (reservable online) | Open — book online |
| Sunday | 8:00 AM – 11:00 PM* | Rental-priority (reservable online) | Open — book online |

*Hours illustrative; confirm final open/close times with the Board.*

**Access rules**
- No public entry during NIA hours or the turnover buffer. Doors open to the public no earlier than 5:00 PM on weekdays.
- Prayer times take precedence over any activity. Activity may pause briefly; renters are notified of expected prayer windows at booking.
- Access is granted only for the reserved space(s) and time(s). Doors, mechanical rooms, offices, and prayer halls are off-limits unless expressly included.
- The Facilities Manager (or designee) or a staff monitor must be on-site whenever the facility is open to renters.
- Minors must be supervised by the renting adult/organization at all times.

### 3.4 Permitted & Prohibited Uses

**3.4.1 Generally permitted (with a proper booking)**
- Basketball, volleyball, futsal/indoor soccer, badminton, and similar court sports; practices, clinics, leagues, and tournaments.
- Youth programs, camps, scouting, and educational/enrichment activities.
- Community gatherings, lectures, fundraisers, banquets, and cultural events.
- Weddings (nikah), walimah/receptions, and family celebrations consistent with Center values.
- Health fairs, blood drives, and civic/nonprofit meetings.

**3.4.2 Prohibited at all times**
- Alcohol, illegal drugs, and smoking/vaping (including hookah) — anywhere on ICN Bellevue property.
- Gambling and games of chance for money.
- Firearms and weapons (except sworn law-enforcement/licensed security engaged for the event).
- Open flame, pyrotechnics, fog/haze machines, or cooking on the gym floor without written approval and any required Fire Marshal permit.
- Any activity, music, performance, imagery, or attire that is sexually explicit, intoxicant-themed, hateful, or otherwise incompatible with the values of an Islamic center.
- Activities that damage the floor (street shoes on the sport floor when prohibited, staking, dragging heavy equipment, adhesives).
- Subletting or transferring the reservation to a third party.
- Exceeding the posted occupancy or blocking exits.

**3.4.3 Restricted — require Board approval**
- Political campaign events, partisan rallies, or candidate fundraisers — reviewed for neutrality and nonprofit-status impact; if permitted, no endorsement is implied and equal-access principles apply.
- Ticketed public events, concerts, or large commercial productions (added security/insurance).
- Amplified sound beyond normal PA use, or events running near closing time.
- Cooking/food preparation on-site (vs. delivered/warmed food) — see food & kitchen rules and Health permits.

**3.4.4 Special considerations for an Islamic Center**
- **Prayer times**: activities yield to congregational prayer; renters are given expected prayer windows and must allow brief pauses and quiet during adhan/prayer if it coincides.
- **Modesty & conduct**: attire and behavior should be respectful of a house of worship; the FM may ask that content be adjusted.
- **Gender-sensitive / private use**: on request, ICN Bellevue will accommodate gender-separated seating or women-only/men-only events where feasible (e.g., screening, separate entrances, female staff/security for women-only events). Note such requests at booking so staffing can be arranged.
- **Music & entertainment**: some renters/events may request no music or specific limits; ICN Bellevue can accommodate and will honor the Center's general guidance on entertainment.
- **Food**: only halal-appropriate service is expected; pork and alcohol are prohibited in any catering, and vendors must be informed.

### 3.5 Health, Safety & Code Compliance

**3.5.1 Occupancy**
- The legally posted occupant-load placard is the controlling limit and must be visible in the space.
- Until the placard is issued, use the planning ceiling (300 seated banquet / 450 standing) and do not advertise capacities above it.
- The renter is responsible for staying within the permitted count for their layout; the FM may stop entry when the limit is reached.

**3.5.2 Egress & fire safety**
- Exits, aisles, and exit signage must remain clear and illuminated at all times.
- No decorations blocking exits; no locking/chaining doors during occupancy.
- Fire extinguishers, alarm pulls, and any sprinkler controls must remain accessible.
- Any candles, cooking, or open flame requires prior approval and any Fire Marshal permit.

**3.5.3 ADA / accessibility**
- Maintain accessible entry, an accessible restroom, and accessible seating/paths of travel.
- Do not place equipment or seating that blocks accessible routes.
- Provide reasonable accommodation on request; note accessibility needs at booking.

**3.5.4 First aid, CPR & AED**
- A stocked first-aid kit and an AED must be on-site and accessible; staff on duty should know their locations.
- At least one staff member per event with current First Aid/CPR/AED certification is recommended; required for events over 100 attendees and for youth sports.
- Injuries/incidents are logged on the Incident Report Form and, if serious, 911 is called first.

*Verify against code before adoption: IFC/IBC assembly-occupancy provisions, NFPA 101 Life Safety Code egress, and Metro Nashville amendments via the Fire Marshal — these determine exits, capacity, and alarm/sprinkler needs.*

### 3.6 Security Staffing Requirements

Security is arranged by ICN Bellevue (using approved licensed personnel or off-duty officers) and billed to the renter. Thresholds below are recommendations based on common venue practice; the FM may raise requirements for higher-risk events (late hours, cash handling, public ticketing, youth safety).

| Event Size / Type | Security Required | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Under 50, low-risk (practice, small meeting) | None required (staff monitor present) | Low crowd/risk; FM/monitor sufficient. |
| 50–150 attendees | 1 security officer | Crowd management, door control. |
| 151–300 attendees | 2 security officers | Two egress/entry points; larger crowd. |
| 301+ attendees | 3+ officers (~1 per 150, min 3) | Multiple zones; parking/traffic. |
| Public ticket sales / open to public | +1 officer over baseline | Unknown attendees; screening. |
| Youth-focused events | Baseline + verified supervision ratios | Child-safety and supervision. |
| Events ending after 10:00 PM | +1 officer | Late-hour risk, parking-lot coverage. |
| Women-only events (on request) | Female security/monitor where feasible | Modesty/comfort accommodation. |

Security staffing surcharge is billed at cost per officer-hour (see §3.13). ICN Bellevue approves all security vendors.

### 3.7 Insurance & Indemnification
- Commercial, ticketed, large private (weddings/receptions), and higher-risk events must provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) with Commercial General Liability of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate.
- Small, low-risk member/community bookings (e.g., a practice under 50 people) may use a lower limit or a day-of/event policy (e.g., TULIP) at the FM's discretion; ICN Bellevue may offer to facilitate event insurance.
- The COI must name "Islamic Center of Nashville at Bellevue Mosque" (and its officers, directors, employees, and volunteers) as Additional Insured for the event date(s).
- Liquor liability is not applicable (alcohol prohibited). If any cooking/catering vendor is used, the vendor should carry its own liability and, for on-site cooking, any required permits.
- Every renter signs the hold-harmless / indemnification clause in the rental agreement.

### 3.8 Equipment Use & Storage
- Included equipment (bleachers, hoops, nets, standards, stage/risers, tables, chairs, PA/AV) is used only as intended and only if listed in the booking.
- Retractable bleachers and basketball hoops are operated by ICN Bellevue staff only.
- Volleyball/badminton nets and standards are set up/removed by staff or trained renters per instruction; no forcing or improvising anchors.
- Tables and chairs are counted at load-in and load-out; the renter is responsible for damage or shortages.
- AV/PA and stage lighting use requires a brief orientation; a tech staffer is required for complex AV.
- All equipment is returned to designated storage; nothing is left on the floor or stacked against exits.
- Renter-supplied equipment must be pre-approved, must not damage floors/walls, and must be removed at load-out.

### 3.9 Setup/Teardown, Load-In/Load-Out & Ancillary Spaces
- Reserved time must include the renter's own setup and teardown; the space must be fully vacated by the end of the reserved window.
- Load-in/out uses designated doors only; no propping fire doors; carts/dollies protect floors.
- The sport floor is protected: approved floor covering is required under tables/chairs for banquets; only non-marking footwear on the playing surface when required.
- Warming/prep kitchen (if booked): for reheating and serving delivered food; on-site cooking requires approval and any Health/Fire permits. Leave clean; commercial cooking is not assumed.
- Restrooms are shared; renters keep them tidy and report issues.
- **Wudu area**: this is a religious ablution space. It is not to be used as a mop sink, food-prep, or general washroom, and must be kept clean and respected. Access only if included and only for ablution.
- Prayer hall / mosque interior is not part of any rental and is off-limits unless separately arranged for prayer use.

### 3.10 Cleaning, Waste Disposal & Hygiene
- Standard turnover cleaning (routine floor/restroom reset) is performed by ICN Bellevue custodial staff; a cleaning fee is included in most rentals to cover it.
- The renter must leave the space "broom clean": all trash bagged and moved to designated dumpsters/points, spills addressed, decorations and personal items removed, tables/chairs cleared.
- Excessive mess, stains, or specialty cleaning (e.g., heavy food events) incurs an additional cleaning charge against the deposit.
- No dumping of grease, food waste, or chemicals in wudu or restroom sinks; kitchen grease is bagged/disposed properly.
- Recycling and waste are separated where bins are provided.
- For food events, the renter provides its own catering cleanup and food-safe handling; the FM may require a larger deposit.

### 3.11 Code of Conduct, Prayer Accommodation & Signage

**Code of conduct**
- Treat the facility as a house of worship's community space: respectful language, dress, and behavior.
- No alcohol, drugs, smoking/vaping, gambling, weapons, harassment, or discrimination.
- Follow all staff/security directions; the FM may pause or end an event for safety or rule violations.
- Supervise children; keep noise within permitted limits; respect neighbors and parking rules.

**Prayer accommodation**
- At booking, ICN Bellevue provides the day's expected prayer (salah) windows so renters can plan around brief pauses.
- If a congregational prayer coincides with an event, the renter allows a short quiet pause and unobstructed access to prayer space/wudu for those who wish to pray.
- The mosque's prayer schedule and religious observances always take precedence over event programming.

**Required signage**
- Posted at entrances and in the gym: occupancy placard; "No Alcohol, No Drugs, No Smoking/Vaping"; "Respect Prayer Times"; emergency exits/evacuation map; first-aid/AED location; and house rules.
- Bilingual (English + Arabic) signage is provided for key items.

### 3.12 Emergency Procedures & Closure Policy

**Emergencies**
- Life-threatening emergencies: call 911 first, then notify the FM/staff. Use the AED/first aid as trained.
- Fire/alarm: evacuate via nearest exit to the posted assembly point; do not re-enter until cleared.
- Severe weather (tornado warning): move to the designated interior shelter area; follow staff.
- All incidents are documented on the Incident Report Form.

**Closures & force majeure**
- ICN Bellevue may close the facility for weather, public-health orders, utility/system failures, security threats, or religious necessity.
- If ICN Bellevue closes and cancels a booking for such reasons, the renter receives a full refund of fees paid or a rescheduled date at no penalty; ICN Bellevue is not liable for the renter's other costs.
- Force majeure (events beyond either party's control) excuses performance; parties work in good faith to reschedule or refund.

### 3.14 Reservation & Approval Workflow

Weekends are rental-priority and must be reservable through an online reservation system. This workflow applies to all public bookings; ICN and NIA programs are entered by staff and hold priority over public requests.

**Booking windows, lead times & blackouts**

| Parameter | Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum lead time | 7 days before event (14 days if security/COI required) | Time to approve, staff, and verify insurance. |
| Maximum advance | Up to 12 months ahead | Allows planning without over-committing the calendar. |
| Weekday public window | 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM only | NIA + turnover buffer protected. |
| Weekend window | Open hours (e.g., 8 AM – 11 PM), rental-priority | Maximize rental use. |
| Blackout dates | Major Islamic holidays (Ramadan schedule, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha), ICN signature events, NIA calendar conflicts, and posted maintenance days | Religious/mission priority and upkeep. |

*ICN Bellevue publishes the current blackout calendar in the reservation system each year. Prayer times are not blackouts but always take precedence during events.*

**Online reservation system — required workflow**
1. Renter creates an account and selects date/time and space; the calendar shows only open, eligible slots (NIA hours, buffers, blackouts are blocked automatically).
2. Renter completes the request form and uploads required attachments.
3. System calculates an estimated quote (base + fees + any security/COI flags) and collects the non-refundable booking fee to hold the date (soft hold pending approval).
4. FM reviews for eligibility, conflicts, staffing, and rule fit; approves, requests changes, or declines within the confirmation timeline (below).
5. On approval, the system issues the rental agreement for e-signature and a payment schedule (deposit + balance).
6. Renter e-signs, pays the deposit, and uploads COI if required; booking becomes Confirmed.
7. Automated reminders go out (balance due date, COI due date, event-day instructions).
8. After the event, staff complete the deposit/refund checklist; the system refunds the deposit less any charges.

**Reservation form fields**
- Organization/individual name and type (member / nonprofit / commercial / private).
- Primary contact: name, phone, email; on-site day-of contact if different.
- Event type and short description; whether open to the public or ticketed.
- Requested date(s), start/end times (including setup/teardown), and space(s)/kitchen needed.
- Expected attendance (and whether youth-focused or women-only/men-only requested).
- Catering/food plans (delivered vs on-site cooking), AV/stage needs, and any special requests (gender-separated seating, accessibility).
- Insurance: acknowledgment and COI upload (if applicable).
- Payment details (card/ACH) and billing info.
- Required attachments: COI (if applicable), nonprofit proof (if claiming nonprofit rate), signed agreement, and any permits.
- Acknowledgment checkboxes: no alcohol/drugs/smoking; respect prayer times; occupancy and conduct rules; cancellation policy.

**Approval steps, timeline & payment rules**

| Stage | Action | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Submission | Renter submits request + booking fee (soft hold) | Immediate |
| FM review | Eligibility, conflicts, staffing, COI/security flags | Within 3 business days |
| Confirmation | Agreement issued for e-signature | Same day as approval |
| Deposit | 50% of estimated total (or flat deposit) to confirm | Within 5 days of approval |
| COI due (if required) | Upload valid COI naming ICN Bellevue | ≥ 10 days before event |
| Final balance | Remaining balance due | 7 days before event |
| Deposit refund | Post-event checklist → refund | Within 14 days after event |

**Cancellation, rescheduling & refunds**

| Notice Before Event | Refund of Fees (excl. booking fee) | Reschedule Option |
|---|---|---|
| 30+ days | Full refund of paid rental fees; deposit returned | Free reschedule (subject to availability) |
| 14–29 days | 50% of rental fees refunded; deposit returned | Reschedule for a small admin fee ($25) |
| 7–13 days | 25% of rental fees refunded; deposit returned | Reschedule at FM discretion |
| Under 7 days / no-show | No refund of rental fees; deposit returned if no damage | No |
| ICN Bellevue cancels (closure/force majeure) | Full refund OR free reschedule | Yes, no penalty |

*The non-refundable booking fee is never refunded (it holds the date). Example: a $190 nonprofit half-day cancelled at 20 days = $95 refunded, $50 booking fee kept, $500 deposit returned. Rescheduling is preferred over cancellation and is offered first.*

**Prioritizing weekend rentals & handling conflicts**
1. Priority order when requests conflict: (1) ICN religious/mission use, (2) NIA contractual needs, (3) ICN community programs, (4) confirmed rentals (first-confirmed wins), (5) new rental requests.
2. Weekends default to rental-priority: hold weekend slots open for rentals rather than routine internal meetings that can occur on weekdays.
3. If ICN/NIA must reclaim a slot already confirmed for a renter (rare), the renter gets a full refund or priority free reschedule, plus a goodwill credit at FM discretion.
4. The system prevents double-booking and shows real-time availability; the FM manages any manual overrides with documentation.

### 3.15 Staffing & Operations Standards

**Minimum staffing by event size/type**

| Event Size / Type | Facility Mgr / Monitor | Security | Custodial | Tech / AV |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Practice / small (< 50) | 1 monitor | 0 | On-call | 0 |
| 50–150 | 1 FM/monitor | 1 | 1 | As needed |
| 151–300 | 1 FM + 1 monitor | 2 | 1–2 | 1 (if AV) |
| 301+ / ticketed / public | 1 FM + 2 monitors | 3+ | 2 | 1+ |
| Youth programs | 1 monitor + verified ratios | Per §3.6 | On-call | 0 |
| Women-only (on request) | Female monitor where feasible | Female where feasible | On-call | As needed |

*A Facilities Manager or trained monitor is present whenever renters are on-site. Security and tech are billed to the renter; custodial turnover is covered by the cleaning fee.*

**Keys, access control & after-hours**
- No keys or access codes are given to renters. ICN Bellevue staff open and secure the building.
- Use zoned access so renters reach only their reserved spaces; prayer halls, offices, and mechanical rooms stay locked.
- Track who opens/closes each event; log arm/disarm of alarm and final lock-up.
- After-hours events end at the posted closing time; staff perform a full walk-through and secure all doors, lights, and HVAC setback.
- Lost-and-found items are logged and held for 30 days.

**Staff training requirements**
- First Aid / CPR / AED: at least one certified staff member on duty per event (required for 100+ and youth events).
- Emergency procedures: evacuation, severe-weather sheltering, and incident reporting.
- Harassment prevention and respectful-conduct training, including cultural/religious sensitivity for a mosque setting.
- Conflict de-escalation and crowd management for monitors and security.
- Facility systems: bleachers, hoops, AV, HVAC/lighting basics, and lockup.
- Annual refresher; new staff trained before their first solo shift; training log maintained.

**Infractions & penalties**

| Excess mess beyond turnover | Extra cleaning fee from deposit | Full deposit loss |
| Property/equipment damage | Repair/replace cost from deposit (+ balance billed) | Loss of privileges until paid |
| Exceeding occupancy / blocking exits | Warning + immediate correction | Event stopped; deposit loss |
| Alcohol / drugs / smoking on property | Event stopped immediately; full deposit loss | Ban from future rentals |
| Unapproved use / subletting | Warning or event stopped; deposit loss | Suspension of privileges |
| Disrespect of prayer/religious space or conduct rules | Warning + correction | Event ended; deposit loss |
| Harassment / violence / weapons | Event stopped; possible 911; deposit loss | Permanent ban; legal referral |
| Fraudulent info / bad-faith booking | Booking voided; fees forfeited | Ban |

*Safety and prohibited-substance violations are zero-tolerance and can end an event immediately without refund. Penalties are documented and communicated in writing.*

**Appeals process**
1. The renter submits a written appeal to the Facilities Manager within 10 business days of the decision or penalty, stating the basis and any evidence.
2. The FM acknowledges within 3 business days and may resolve directly or refer to the Board/Facilities Committee.
3. If referred, the Board/Committee reviews at its next meeting (or within 30 days) and issues a written decision.
4. The Board decision is final. Documentation of the appeal and outcome is retained.

## 4. Procedures
1. FM confirms the posted occupant-load placard with the Fire Marshal before advertising any capacity.
2. FM publishes the annual blackout calendar (holidays, ICN events, NIA conflicts, maintenance days) before the reservation system opens each year.
3. All public bookings flow through the online reservation workflow in §3.14, with no exceptions for informal/manual bookings.
4. FM verifies COI and security requirements are met before a booking is marked Confirmed.
5. Post-event, FM completes the deposit/refund checklist within 14 days and logs any infractions per §3.16.
6. Rates are reviewed each spring per §3.13 and any change is approved by the Board and communicated 30–60 days in advance.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facilities Manager (FM)**: Owns scheduling, approvals, day-of operations, staffing assignment, deposit/refund processing, and infraction documentation.
- **Facility Monitor / Staff on Duty**: Present whenever renters are on-site; enforces access, conduct, and safety rules.
- **Security Vendor (ICN-approved)**: Provides staffing per §3.6 thresholds, billed to the renter.
- **Board / Facilities Committee**: Approves pricing, security thresholds, insurance minimums, political-event requests, and hears appeals.
- **Renter / Permittee**: Responsible for their event's conduct, occupancy compliance, cleanup, and any damage or overtime charges.
