## 1. Purpose
To ensure ICN Bellevue maintains adequate insurance coverage and remains compliant with nonprofit, tax, and local permitting requirements.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all insurance policies, nonprofit filings, and local permits held in ICN Bellevue's name.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Insurance Coverage
- **General liability insurance** is maintained at a level appropriate for a facility hosting gatherings of up to 1,000 people and regular public events, reviewed annually with the insurance broker.
- **Directors & Officers (D&O) insurance** is maintained to protect Board members from personal liability related to their governance duties.
- **Workers' compensation insurance** is maintained for any paid staff, as required by Tennessee law.
- **Property insurance** covers the building, contents, and any owned vehicles.

### 3.2 Contractor & Renter Insurance
- Any outside contractor performing work on-site, and any outside renter hosting a large event, must provide a Certificate of Insurance naming ICN Bellevue as additionally insured, per the thresholds set in the Facility Maintenance and Community Services policies.

### 3.3 Nonprofit Compliance
- ICN Bellevue maintains its 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, with all required annual filings (e.g., IRS Form 990) completed on time.
- State nonprofit registration and any required charitable-solicitation registration with the State of Tennessee are kept current.

### 3.4 Local Permits
- ICN Bellevue maintains a valid assembly occupancy certificate reflecting its actual posted occupancy.
- Any food service holds the required health department permit.
- Sound system operation complies with the local noise ordinance (see Prayer & Worship Operations Policy).

## 4. Procedures
1. Treasurer/Legal Committee reviews all insurance policies annually before renewal, adjusting coverage as the facility or programs grow.
2. Certificates of Insurance from contractors/renters are collected and filed before work/events begin.
3. Nonprofit filings (Form 990, state registrations) are tracked on a compliance calendar with reminders at least 60 days before each deadline.
4. Local permits are verified as current annually and posted as required.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Board Treasurer**: Owns the insurance renewal process and compliance calendar.
- **Legal Committee**: Reviews nonprofit filings and permit compliance, engages legal counsel as needed.
- **Facility Manager**: Collects and files contractor/renter insurance certificates.
