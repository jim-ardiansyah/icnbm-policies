## 1. Purpose
To establish clear expectations for staff and volunteer roles, training, conduct, and safety — particularly for anyone working with children.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all paid staff, contract staff, and volunteers of ICN Bellevue.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Job Descriptions & Reporting Lines
- Written job descriptions are maintained for the Imam, Facility Manager, custodial staff, front-desk/admin staff, and Youth Director, each with a clear reporting line to the Executive Committee (EC).

### 3.2 Volunteer Policy
- All volunteers complete a brief application and orientation before taking on a recurring role.
- Volunteers working with children, security, or handling funds must pass a criminal background check before starting.
- Volunteers sign in for scheduled shifts to maintain an accurate coverage record.

### 3.3 Background Checks
- Mandatory for: youth-program staff/volunteers, security team members, and any role with unsupervised access to children or cash handling.
- Renewed every 2–3 years or per insurer requirements, whichever is more frequent.

### 3.4 Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment
- All staff and volunteers are expected to treat congregants, colleagues, and visitors with respect and dignity.
- Harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate removal from duties.
- A confidential process for reporting concerns is available to all staff and volunteers (see Section 3.5).

### 3.5 Grievance Procedure
- Staff or volunteers may raise concerns to the Executive Committee (EC) or, if the concern involves the Executive Committee (EC), directly to the Executive Committee Chair.
- All grievances are documented and reviewed confidentially within 10 business days.

### 3.6 Training Requirements
- Youth-facing staff/volunteers: mandatory reporting (child safety) training and the Two-Adult Rule (see Children, Youth & Family Safety Policy).
- Security-facing staff/volunteers: active-threat response training.
- Designated first responders: current CPR/First Aid certification.

## 4. Procedures
1. HR Committee maintains current job descriptions and reviews annually.
2. New volunteers complete application, orientation, and (if applicable) background check before their first unsupervised shift.
3. Training completions logged in a central roster with renewal dates tracked.
4. Grievances logged confidentially and reviewed per the timeline above.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Executive Committee (EC)**: Manages staff, oversees hiring, first point of contact for grievances.
- **HR Committee**: Maintains job descriptions, training roster, and background-check records.
- **Executive Committee Chair**: Handles grievances involving the Executive Committee (EC).
