## 1. Purpose

To protect the safety and security of worshippers, staff, volunteers, visitors, and property at ICN Bellevue through continuous camera surveillance, while ensuring that recorded footage is accessed only when necessary, only by authorized individuals, and in a manner that respects the privacy and dignity of every member of the community, especially sisters.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all camera surveillance equipment, live feeds, and recorded footage at ICN Bellevue, covering all interior and exterior areas of the premises, including prayer halls, hallways, classrooms, offices, entrances, parking lots, and grounds. It applies to all staff, volunteers, Trustees, the Imam, contractors, and any individual who operates, maintains, views, or requests access to the camera system or its recordings.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Coverage & Monitoring

- All interior and exterior areas of ICN Bellevue are monitored and recorded by camera **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**.
- **Privacy-protected areas are never recorded.** No camera may be placed inside restrooms, wudu (ablution) areas, changing areas, or any space where individuals have a reasonable expectation of privacy. Cameras may cover the entrances to these areas only.
- The system records **video only**. Audio recording is not enabled unless separately approved by the Executive Committee (EC) and the Board of Trustees.
- Clear signage stating that the premises are under 24/7 video surveillance is posted at all public entrances.

### 3.2 Authorized Access

- Access to live feeds and recorded footage is **limited to individuals appointed in writing by the Executive Committee (EC)** ("Authorized Camera Personnel").
- The EC maintains a current list of Authorized Camera Personnel and reviews it at least annually, and whenever a role changes.
- No other staff, volunteer, Trustee, or community member may view, copy, download, or share footage, regardless of position.
- System logins are individual (no shared passwords), and access is removed immediately when an appointment ends.

### 3.3 Approval Required for Recording Review

- **Any review of recorded footage requires prior approval from the Executive Committee (EC).** This includes reviews requested by staff, volunteers, Trustees, the Imam, or community members.
- The EC approves a review only for a legitimate purpose, such as a safety or security incident, property damage or theft, an injury or accident, a Code of Conduct concern, or a lawful request from law enforcement.
- Approval is limited to the specific incident, date, time range, and camera locations needed. Browsing footage beyond the approved scope is prohibited.
- In an emergency posing an immediate threat to life or safety, Authorized Camera Personnel may view footage at once and must notify the EC Chair as soon as possible, and in any case within 24 hours.

### 3.4 Incidents Involving Sisters

- Any incident requiring footage review that **involves one or more sisters** is reviewed by an **independent sister appointed by the Executive Committee (EC)** ("Appointed Sister Reviewer").
- The Appointed Sister Reviewer must have no personal involvement in, or relationship with the parties to, the incident under review. If she has a conflict of interest, the EC appoints an alternate sister reviewer.
- Male Authorized Camera Personnel may assist only with technical retrieval of footage (locating and exporting the approved time range) and may not view the content of footage covering the sisters' prayer hall or other sisters-only areas.
- The Appointed Sister Reviewer reports her findings in writing to the EC, sharing only what is necessary to address the incident. Footage itself is not shared further without EC approval.

### 3.5 Retention & Security of Footage

- Recordings are retained for **30 days** *\[EC to confirm based on system capacity\]* and then automatically overwritten.
- Footage related to an approved incident review, a legal matter, or a law enforcement request is preserved until the matter is closed, as directed by the EC.
- Recording equipment and storage are kept in a locked, secure location, and remote access is protected by strong passwords and multi-factor authentication where available, per the IT, AV & Communications Policy (ICN-13).

### 3.6 Law Enforcement & External Requests

- Requests for footage from law enforcement, insurers, attorneys, or any outside party are referred to the EC and, where relevant, legal counsel before any footage is released.
- Footage is released to law enforcement only with EC approval, or in response to a valid subpoena, warrant, or court order.

### 3.7 Prohibited Uses

- Footage may not be used to monitor personal matters, attendance, or the private conduct of community members unrelated to an approved incident.
- Footage may not be posted on social media, shared with the media, or circulated by phone, messaging app, or any other means, except as approved under this policy and the Media & Public Statement Policy (ICN-19).
- Recording screens with a personal phone or camera is strictly prohibited.
- Any violation of this policy may result in removal of access, removal from volunteer or staff duties, and other action under the Community Code of Conduct Policy (ICN-14).

## 4. Procedures

1. **Request.** Anyone needing a footage review submits a request to the Executive Committee (EC), stating the incident, date, approximate time, location, and reason for review.
2. **EC decision.** The EC approves or declines the request, defines the approved scope (time range and cameras), and names the reviewer. If the incident involves a sister, the EC assigns the Appointed Sister Reviewer.
3. **Retrieval.** Authorized Camera Personnel retrieve only the approved footage and preserve a copy if the incident may lead to further action.
4. **Review.** The assigned reviewer views the footage within the approved scope only. Where a sister is involved, the review is conducted solely by the Appointed Sister Reviewer.
5. **Report.** The reviewer submits a brief written summary of findings to the EC.
6. **Log.** Every request, approval, review, export, and release of footage is recorded in a Footage Access Log (date, requester, approver, reviewer, cameras and time range, purpose, outcome), kept by the EC.
7. **Closure.** When the matter is resolved, the EC directs whether preserved footage is retained further or deleted.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Executive Committee (EC)**: Owns this policy; appoints Authorized Camera Personnel and the Appointed Sister Reviewer; approves or declines every footage review and release; maintains the Footage Access Log.
- **Authorized Camera Personnel**: Operate and maintain the system; retrieve approved footage; never view or share footage outside an approved scope.
- **Appointed Sister Reviewer**: Independently reviews footage of any incident involving sisters and reports findings to the EC in confidence.
- **IT/Communications Coordinator**: Supports system security, user accounts, and storage per ICN-13.
- **Board of Trustees**: Approves this policy, any expansion such as audio recording, and any matter with legal or reputational risk.
