## 1. Purpose
To ensure donations — especially Zakat — are collected, tracked, and disbursed with financial integrity, transparency, and compliance with Shariah requirements.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all donation collection (cash boxes, online giving, event fundraising), fund accounting, and financial reporting at ICN Bellevue.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Cash Handling
- Jummah and Eid donation boxes are sealed and counted by at least two unrelated individuals (dual control) immediately after collection.
- Counted funds are deposited to the bank within 24–48 hours.
- A count log is signed by both counters and retained.

### 3.2 Zakat Fund Segregation
- Zakat (obligatory charity) funds are tracked in a separate ledger/account from general Sadaqah (voluntary donation) and operating funds, in accordance with Shariah requirements that Zakat be used only for its prescribed categories of recipients.
- Zakat disbursements are approved by the Finance/Zakat Committee against documented need, with recipient records kept confidential but auditable.

### 3.3 Donor Privacy
- Donor contact and giving information is stored securely and used only for receipting, communication, and internal reporting — never sold or shared with outside parties.

### 3.4 Annual Audit / Financial Review
- ICN Bellevue's finances are reviewed annually by an independent auditor or qualified financial review committee.
- A summary of income, expenses, and Zakat disbursement is shared with the community (e.g., at the Annual General Body meeting) for transparency.

### 3.5 Budget Approval
- An annual operating budget, including the capital reserve allocation (see Facility Maintenance Policy), is prepared by the Finance Committee and approved by the Board before the fiscal year begins.

## 4. Procedures
1. Two designated counters open and count each donation box after every Jummah, Taraweeh night, and Eid, logging the amount and signing the count sheet.
2. Treasurer deposits funds within 48 hours and records the transaction against the correct fund (General/Sadaqah vs. Zakat).
3. Zakat disbursement requests are reviewed and approved by the Finance/Zakat Committee before funds are released.
4. Annual audit/review scheduled each year, with results presented to the Board and community.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Treasurer**: Manages bank deposits, fund ledgers, and budget tracking.
- **Finance/Zakat Committee**: Approves Zakat disbursements, oversees the annual audit.
- **Board of Directors**: Approves the annual budget and reviews audit results.
