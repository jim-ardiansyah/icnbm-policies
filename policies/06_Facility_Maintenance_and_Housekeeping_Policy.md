## 1. Purpose
To keep the ICN Bellevue building and grounds safe, clean, and in good working order through preventive maintenance, cleaning standards, and disciplined vendor management.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all interior and exterior areas of the facility, all maintenance staff, custodial staff, and outside contractors.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Preventive Maintenance
- HVAC systems: filter changes on a manufacturer-recommended schedule (typically monthly to quarterly); full seasonal servicing each spring and fall.
- Plumbing and electrical systems inspected annually by a licensed contractor.
- Roof inspected annually and after major storms.
- Elevator/lift (if present) certified per state requirements.

### 3.2 Cleaning Standards
- **Daily**: Prayer halls vacuumed/swept, restrooms cleaned and restocked, entrances cleared of debris.
- **Weekly**: Wudu areas deep-cleaned, kitchen deep-cleaned, high-touch surfaces (door handles, railings) disinfected.
- **Monthly**: Carpet spot-treatment, window cleaning (entrances), storage area organization.
- **Seasonal**: Full deep clean of prayer halls and carpets before Ramadan; post-Ramadan deep clean before Eid.

### 3.3 Prayer Hall & Carpet Care
- Shoes-off policy enforced at all hall entrances with clearly marked shoe-storage areas that never block exits.
- Spot-cleaning performed immediately for spills or stains; professional carpet cleaning scheduled at minimum twice yearly.

### 3.4 Pest Control
- Licensed pest control contracted on a quarterly service schedule, with additional visits as needed.

### 3.5 Vendor & Contractor Management
- All contractors must be on the approved vendor list and provide a current Certificate of Insurance before beginning work.
- Work orders are logged with scope, date, and sign-off by the Facility Manager upon completion.

### 3.6 Capital Reserve Planning
- A minimum percentage of the annual operating budget (recommended 5–10%) is allocated to a capital reserve fund for major repairs and replacements (roof, HVAC, parking lot resurfacing, carpet replacement).

### 3.7 Asset Inventory
- A register of major equipment and furnishings (HVAC units, sound/AV system, chairs/tables, kitchen equipment) is maintained with purchase date, warranty status, and expected replacement year.

## 4. Procedures
1. Facility Manager maintains a maintenance calendar covering all recurring inspections and servicing.
2. Custodial staff follow a posted daily/weekly/monthly cleaning checklist, initialed upon completion.
3. Any maintenance issue is logged via a work-order form; urgent issues (life-safety, active leaks) addressed within 24 hours.
4. Vendor Certificates of Insurance verified and filed before any contractor begins work on-site.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Facility Manager**: Owns the maintenance calendar, vendor list, and asset inventory.
- **Custodial Staff**: Execute daily/weekly cleaning checklists.
- **Facilities Committee**: Reviews capital reserve allocation and major repair planning annually.
