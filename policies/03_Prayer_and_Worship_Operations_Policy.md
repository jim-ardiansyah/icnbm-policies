## 1. Purpose
To standardize daily prayer operations, sound system use, worship-space upkeep, and religious life-cycle services (janazah, nikah) so that worship runs consistently and respectfully.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the Imam, muezzin/sound operators, wudu-area attendants, and any staff involved in scheduling or supporting prayer services.

## 3. Policy Statement

### 3.1 Daily Prayer Schedule
- Prayer and Iqamah times are set using an approved athan calculation method and posted at all entrances, on the website, and on the ICN Bellevue app/notification system if available.
- Any adjustment to Iqamah timing (e.g., for Jummah khutbah length) requires Imam approval and advance notice to the community.

### 3.2 Imam & Khateeb Scheduling
- The Imam delivers regular khutbahs; guest khateebs must be vetted and approved in advance by the Religious Affairs Committee.
- A backup khateeb list is maintained for Imam absence.

### 3.3 Sound System / Athan Policy
- Internal (hall) speaker volume is set for clarity without distortion; external speaker (athan) volume complies with the local municipal noise ordinance.
- Sound system tested weekly before Jummah; maintenance log kept.

### 3.4 Women's Prayer Area
- The women's section is maintained with sightline and audio quality equivalent to the men's section.
- A dedicated entrance is provided where the building layout allows, with seating positioned near childcare/family areas.

### 3.5 Wudu (Ablution) Areas
- Wudu stations are cleaned and inspected before each daily prayer, with an intensive clean before Jummah.
- Non-slip mats maintained at all wet-floor areas; water heaters serviced per manufacturer schedule.
- Low-flow fixtures used to manage water consumption.

### 3.6 Janazah (Funeral) Services
- Families requesting janazah services contact the Imam's office; scheduling coordinated with the funeral home and, if applicable, the ghusl (washing) room.
- If body storage/refrigeration is offered on-site, it is maintained under a documented health and safety protocol.

### 3.7 Nikah & Religious Ceremonies
- Nikah bookings go through the standard facility booking process (see Community Services & Event Management Policy) with officiant and documentation requirements confirmed by the Imam's office.

## 4. Procedures
1. Prayer schedule finalized monthly and posted at least one week in advance.
2. Guest khateeb requests submitted to Religious Affairs Committee at least two weeks ahead of the intended date.
3. Sound technician performs weekly test and logs results.
4. Janazah requests logged with date/time, family contact, and any special requirements.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities
- **Imam**: Leads worship, approves guest khateebs, oversees janazah/nikah religious requirements.
- **Facility Manager**: Maintains wudu areas and sound system.
- **Religious Affairs Committee**: Vets guest speakers, resolves fiqh-related scheduling questions.
